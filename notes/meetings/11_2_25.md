## Strategy Search 
- Have model generate strategies for a proof given a goal
 
- Then perform 2-level hierarchical search
  - First level: search over strategies
  - Second level: Search over tactics/proofs given strategy

- Training data can be obtained retrospectively with foundation model, as done by Lean-STaR
  - Instead of step-by-step, we want high level strategies
  - Then have data to train both strategy model and tactic model 

- Qs:
  - How to deal with proofs requiring context/lemmas?
  - Search strategies beyond BFS
    - Diversity over strategies (DPP?)
    - Budget based (e.g. strategy includes a budget for each subgoal)
    - Novelty compared to Lean-STaR: Strategies rather than step by step, plus search
    - How to decompose strategy? Do we have one high level strategy, or each subgoal with its own strategy as well?
      - Strategies with subgoals is true 2-level search, but harder to train
  - Learning from failed attempts? 
    - Use foundation model again to generate alternative strategy, given a failure?
    - "Go Back" action

      - Some kind of 'has the goal made progress' or 'is it consistent with the strategy'
