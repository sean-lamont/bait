- Will filter minimum of (k, trace(sim_matrix))
  - Expected value of a sample from DPP is trace(sim_matrix)
  - More similar matrices give lower trace, and vice versa
  - Testing with k = 2, 8, 16
    - k = 16 works best so far, doing slightly better than normal bestfs 
  - trace(sim_matrix) is quite small generally 
  - Problem: since it's quite small (and doesn't actually vary much), we're filtering too many tactics without a strict minimum
  - Hence higher strict minimum seems to work better
